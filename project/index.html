Welcome to Tune

Tune is my personal music blog where I share honest album and artist reviews and build playlists for specific moods and activities, like hiking, reading, homework, and road trips.

Every playlist is built from songs on the albums I review, so you can read my take on a record and then listen to the best parts of it in the setting that fits. Start with a review or jump straight into a playlist.
