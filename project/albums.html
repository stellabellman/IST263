Sweet Boy

Malcolm Todd

9/10

Sweet Boy is Malcolm Todd's debut mixtape, released April 5, 2024 on Columbia Records. It's the project that took him from TikTok buzz to a major-label deal. The 16 songs run about 45 minutes and pull together his earlier Sweet Boy Pt. 1 and Pt. 2 EPs along with fan favorites like "Art House," "Roommates," "Hot in NY," and "Mr. Incorrect."

The sound sits between indie pop, R&B, and a little bit of rock, and most of the songs are about young love, nerves, and being a little too in your feelings. What I like most is how relaxed and personal it feels. The grooves are smooth, the hooks stick, and the lyrics sound like someone telling you about their week rather than trying to impress you. It's easy to put on and just as easy to replay.

Track-by-track Ratings

Track ratings for Sweet Boy
Track Rating My take
Earrings 9/10 The perfect opener. Todd admits right away that he's stuck in his feelings, and the laid-back groove makes it easy to settle into the rest of the project.
Art House 10/10 One of the songs that made people pay attention to him. It's catchy and a little moody, and it's the one I'd play for someone who has never heard of him.
Sweet Boy 8/10 The title track first came out in May 2023, before the mixtape existed. It's playful and sweet, and it captures the personality the whole project is built around.

Overall Verdict

Sweet Boy doesn't try to reinvent indie pop or R&B, and it doesn't need to. It's fun, honest, and full of songs that sound great on a sunny afternoon or a late-night drive. A few tracks blur together near the end, but the highs are high enough that I'd still call it one of the most replayable projects I've heard from a new artist. I'm giving it a 9/10.
